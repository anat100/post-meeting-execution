# Anat Gilad — voice guide

> **Status: test draft.** This was written as a best guess, not from Anat's real emails. Replace it with patterns from 5–10 follow-ups she has actually sent before relying on it.

This file covers **how** the email sounds. **What** it says comes from `anatco-followup-scenarios` and the transcript.

---

## Tone

- Warm, direct, peer-to-peer. Writes like a helpful operator, not a salesperson.
- Confident without hype. No superlatives ("game-changing", "best-in-class"), no exclamation marks beyond one per email at most.
- Specific over polite. Names the exact thing the prospect said rather than paraphrasing it into generic benefits.
- Plain English. Avoids jargon the prospect didn't use first; if they used their own term for something, mirror it.

## Greeting and opening

- Greeting: `Hi [First name],` — first names only. For multiple recipients: `Hi [Name] and [Name],` (two people) or `Hi all,` (three or more).
- First line goes straight to substance, usually a short thank-you tied to something specific: "Thanks for walking me through how your team handles inbound routing today."
- Never open with "I hope this email finds you well", "Just following up" or "Great chatting today!".

## Structure and formatting

- Length: 80–180 words for most emails; up to ~250 for trial or contract emails with several owners and dates.
- Short paragraphs (1–3 sentences). Bullets for lists of three or more items, next steps, or owners and dates.
- Bold sparingly — at most one bolded phrase, usually the date or the ask.
- Dates written out with the weekday: "Tuesday, Nov 12". Times include the time zone.
- End with the single ask or next step, phrased as a question when a reply is needed: "Does Thursday at 3pm CET work for the scoping call?"
- **No sign-off block or signature** — Anat's email client adds it. A one-word close like "Best," is fine only if nothing else follows.

## Words and phrases

- Prefers: "next step", "as you mentioned", "to make sure the trial is useful", "happy to".
- Avoids: "circle back", "touch base", "synergy", "leverage", "per my last email", "just checking in", "no worries if not".

## Links

- No pre-approved links are on file yet.
- Include a link only if it was shared or promised in the call **and** the URL appears in the transcript or meeting notes.
- If something was promised but the URL isn't known, write `[LINK: short description]` so Anat can fill it in before sending. Never invent a URL.
- Never add the Fireflies.ai recording or summary link; the notes tool handles that.

## Quick self-check before handing off the draft

1. Could this email have been sent to any other company? If yes, make it more specific.
2. Is the ask in the last line, and is there only one?
3. Any phrase from the "avoids" list, any invented link, or a signature block? Remove it.
